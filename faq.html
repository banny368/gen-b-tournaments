FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about Gen B Tournaments

🚀 GETTING STARTED

How do I create an account?+
Click "Sign Up" on the homepage, enter your username, email and password. You'll receive a verification email — click the link to activate your account. Once verified, you can log in and start playing!
Is Gen B Tournaments free to use?+
Yes! Registration is completely free. We offer both free and paid tournaments. Free tournaments let you practice without any entry fee. Paid tournaments require an entry fee from your wallet but offer real cash prizes.
Which games are supported?+
We currently support BGMI (Battlegrounds Mobile India), Free Fire, and COD Mobile. More games will be added soon based on community demand.

💰 WALLET & PAYMENTS

How do I deposit money?+
Go to Wallet → Deposit. Scan the UPI QR code and pay your desired amount. After payment, enter your UTR (12-digit transaction ID) and upload a screenshot. Our team will approve your deposit within 24 hours. Minimum deposit is ₹50.
How long does deposit approval take?+
Deposits are typically approved within 2–4 hours during business hours (9 AM – 10 PM IST). In rare cases it may take up to 24 hours. Contact support if your deposit is pending for over 24 hours.
How do I withdraw my winnings?+
Go to Wallet → Withdraw. Enter your UPI ID and the amount you wish to withdraw (min ₹100, max ₹10,000 per request). Withdrawals are processed within 24 hours to your UPI account. Only winnings from the Winning Wallet can be withdrawn.
What are the three wallet types?+
Main Wallet: Funded by deposits. Used to pay tournament entry fees.

Bonus Wallet: Earned via rewards, referrals, and daily spin. Used in free tournaments only — cannot be withdrawn.

Winning Wallet: Prize money from tournament wins. This is the only balance that can be withdrawn to your bank/UPI.
My deposit was rejected. What should I do?+
Common reasons: incorrect UTR, duplicate submission, or unclear screenshot. Please re-submit with the correct UTR from your UPI app's transaction history, or contact support on Telegram/WhatsApp with your payment screenshot.

🏆 TOURNAMENTS

How do I join a tournament?+
Browse tournaments on the Tournaments page. Click on a tournament to view details. Click "Register Now" — for paid tournaments the entry fee is deducted from your Main Wallet. The Room ID and Password will be published before match time.
When will I get the Room ID and Password?+
Room details are published on the tournament page 15–30 minutes before the match starts. You'll also receive a push notification. Make sure to enable notifications for timely updates.
How are prizes distributed?+
After the tournament ends, our team verifies the results (usually within 1–2 hours). Prize money is credited directly to your Winning Wallet. You can then withdraw it to your UPI account.
Can I cancel my tournament registration?+
Cancellations are allowed up to 1 hour before the tournament starts. Your entry fee will be refunded to your Main Wallet. After that, no refunds are issued.

🎁 REFERRALS & REWARDS

How does the referral system work?+
Share your unique referral link from the Referral page. When a friend signs up using your link and deposits for the first time, you earn ₹10 bonus in your Bonus Wallet. There is no limit to how many friends you can refer!
What is the Daily Spin Wheel?+
Visit the Rewards page every day to spin the wheel once. Prizes include cash (₹5 to ₹100) added to your Bonus Wallet, or XP points that level up your profile. The spin resets every 24 hours.
What are daily login rewards?+
Log in every day to claim your daily reward! Rewards increase over a 7-day streak: Day 1 gives ₹5, Day 7 gives ₹50. Missing a day resets your streak to Day 1.
